About ZippyWidgets

Fast, free tools for everyday tasks — no fluff, no accounts.

What is ZippyWidgets?

ZippyWidgets is a growing collection of free, instant utility tools that run entirely in your browser. No downloads, no sign-ups, no subscriptions — just open a tool and use it. Every widget is built to be fast, mobile-friendly, and accessible to anyone.

Our Mission

The web is full of tools that make you create an account just to count words or generate a QR code. We built ZippyWidgets because useful tools should be instant and friction-free. Everything runs client-side — your data never leaves your device.
