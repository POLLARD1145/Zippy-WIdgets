Why ZippyWidgets?

Every tool on ZippyWidgets is built around one principle: no friction. There are no accounts to create, no email addresses to hand over, no paywalls to navigate, and no apps to download. Open the page, use the tool, done. All processing happens directly in your browser — your text, your dates, your WiFi passwords, and your lists never leave your device.

The six tools here cover the tasks that come up repeatedly in everyday digital life. The Random Picker Wheel is used by teachers, streamers, and event hosts worldwide for fair, transparent selections. The Font Generator produces over 30 Unicode text styles that paste directly into Instagram, TikTok, Discord, and Twitter. The Word Counter tracks not just words but platform-specific character limits for Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, SMS, and more. The QR Code Generator creates WiFi, URL, email, and plain-text QR codes with custom colours and high-resolution PNG export. The Age Calculator gives precise results down to hours lived and days until the next birthday. The Speed Test measures download, upload, ping and jitter against Cloudflare's global network — with plain-English connection quality ratings for gaming, streaming, video calls and more.

All tools are free. All tools work offline after first load. Read the blog for in-depth guides on getting the most out of each one.
