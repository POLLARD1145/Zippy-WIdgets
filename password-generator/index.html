How to Create a Strong Password

Weak passwords are the single most common cause of account takeovers. A strong password is long, random, and unique — and it should never be reused across multiple sites. This generator creates cryptographically random passwords that are extremely difficult to guess or crack.

Password Length Recommendations

  • 12 characters: Minimum recommended for general online accounts.
  • 16 characters: Good balance of security and usability for most passwords.
  • 20+ characters: Recommended for banking, email, and password manager master passwords.
  • 32+ characters: Overkill for humans, but ideal for API keys and machine accounts.

What the Strength Meter Means

The strength meter estimates how long a brute-force attack would take to crack your password based on its length and character variety. Short passwords with only one character type are weak. Long passwords mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols are very strong.

Privacy Guarantee

This password generator uses your browser's built-in Web Crypto API to produce random values. Passwords are generated locally and never leave your device. We do not store, log, or transmit anything.

Pro Tip: Use a Password Manager

Generating a strong password is only half the battle — you also need to store it safely. Use a reputable password manager (Bitwarden, KeePass, 1Password, or your browser's built-in manager) to save and autofill your passwords. That way every account can have a unique, random password without you needing to remember any of them.
