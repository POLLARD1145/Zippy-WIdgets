How to Add a Logo to a QR Code Without Breaking It

When you place a logo in the centre of a QR code, you are covering part of the data modules. Reliable generators compensate by using a higher error-correction level, which builds redundancy into the image so the code still scans even if part of it is hidden.

Tip 1: Keep your logo small. A good rule of thumb is no more than 15-20% of the QR code's total area. Larger logos increase scan failure rates.
Tip 2: Use a simple logo with a transparent background. Complex photographs or detailed illustrations don't reduce well at small sizes.
Tip 3: Always test-scan before printing. Use your phone's native camera app to confirm the code reads reliably from the screen and from a printed test copy.
Tip 4: Maintain contrast. The foreground modules should be significantly darker than the background. Avoid light-grey codes or coloured backgrounds that clash with your logo.

When Should You Pay for a QR Code Generator?

Free generators are enough for most personal and small-business use. Consider upgrading only if you need:

  • Bulk generation of hundreds or thousands of codes at once

For a one-off menu, business card, WiFi sign, or event poster, a free static QR code with your logo is all you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a QR code with a logo for free?

Yes. Tools like ZippyWidgets, QRCode Monkey, and TQRCG let you upload a centre logo and download a clean PNG with no watermark and no account.

What is the best free QR code generator with logo support?

For a fully free, no-sign-up experience, ZippyWidgets is the best choice. For vector print files, QRCode Monkey is better. For dynamic codes with analytics, Hovercode is the pick.

Do free QR codes with logos expire?

Static QR codes do not expire — the data is encoded directly into the image. Dynamic QR codes rely on the provider's redirect service, so they can stop working if the service closes or your account is deleted.

Will a logo make my QR code unscannable?

Not if the generator uses high error correction and you keep the logo reasonably sized. Always test the final image on multiple phones before printing in bulk.
