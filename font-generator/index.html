Frequently Asked Questions

How does the fancy font generator work?

It converts your text into Unicode character equivalents that look like different fonts — no special software needed. Because they’re standard Unicode, they paste anywhere: Instagram bios, TikTok profiles, Twitter, Discord, and more.

How do I change my font on Instagram bio?

Type your text above, choose a style, click “Copy”, then paste directly into your Instagram bio editor. Instagram renders Unicode characters exactly as you see them here.

Do these fonts work on TikTok and Discord?

Yes. Because the characters are Unicode — not images or custom fonts — they display natively on every platform that supports UTF-8, including TikTok, Discord, Twitter/X, Facebook, and WhatsApp.

Why do some characters appear as boxes?

A small number of Unicode blocks (like some mathematical alphanumeric symbols) are not included in every device font. Most modern phones and computers display all styles correctly.
