📖 How to Play

Objective: Defeat all 5 bosses by generating enough Aether Power before your turns run out.

Card Types:
🔥 Fire — generates Aether Power equal to its value (Slots 1–3)
💧 Water — doubles an adjacent Fire card's value (Slots 1–3)
🌿 Earth — carries its value to the next turn if you don't win (Slots 1–3)
⚡ Volt — multiplies total power of all 3 Core slots (Slot 4 only)
🌀 Void — inverts values: cards score (10 − value) × 10 (Slot 4 only)

Scoring Formula:
1. Water doubles adjacent Fire.
2. Sum all 3 Core slots (including Earth carry).
3. Apply Catalyst: Volt multiplies the sum, Void inverts.
4. If total ≥ Boss Defense → Victory!

Daily Sync: Every player worldwide gets the same bosses and card drops today. Compare scores with friends!

Earth Carry: An Earth card left on the board carries its value as a bonus to your next turn automatically.

What is AetherForge: Chrono-Deck?

AetherForge: Chrono-Deck is a free browser roguelike deckbuilder — no login, no download, no signup required. Open the page and play instantly. It is one of the few online card games with no login that offers a fully synchronized global daily challenge: every player in the world faces the exact same 5 bosses, with the same card drops, generated from today's date as a seed.

If you enjoy games like Balatro in the browser, daily puzzle games like Wordle, or classic roguelikes — AetherForge is designed for you. Each run takes 5–15 minutes and resets daily.

How to Play — Game Rules

Card Types

  • 🔥 Fire (Core, Slots 1–3) — generates Aether Power equal to its face value.
  • 💧 Water (Core, Slots 1–3) — doubles the Aether Power of an adjacent Fire card.
  • 🌿 Earth (Core, Slots 1–3) — carries its value forward as a bonus on your next turn if you don't win this turn.
  • ⚡ Volt (Catalyst, Slot 4 only) — multiplies the total power of all three Core slots.
  • 🌀 Void (Catalyst, Slot 4 only) — inverts scoring: low-value cards generate high power.

Turn Loop

  1. Each boss shows a Defense Score and a Turn Limit.
  2. Draw 5 cards from your deck.
  3. Place up to 3 Core cards in Slots 1–3 and an optional Catalyst in Slot 4.
  4. Hit Forge — if your Aether Power meets or beats the Defense Score, the boss falls.
  5. Choose a reward card to grow your deck, then face the next boss.
  6. Run out of turns on any boss and the run ends.

How the Daily Seed Works

AetherForge uses a deterministic pseudo-random number generator (PRNG) seeded by today's UTC date. Because the generation is purely mathematical and runs in your browser, a player in London and a player in Tokyo face identical boss sequences and card drop options on the same calendar day — with no server, no database, and no account required.

Add ?customSeed=12345 to the URL to practice on a specific seed, or share your victory URL (generated on win) so friends can verify your score is legitimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AetherForge free to play?

Yes — completely free, forever. No ads during gameplay, no premium currency, no pay-to-win mechanics.

Do I need to create an account?

No. AetherForge is one of the few no-login web card games. Your stats and run progress are saved locally in your browser using localStorage — nothing is sent to any server.

How often does the daily challenge reset?

Every day at midnight UTC. The seed changes to the new date, generating a fresh set of bosses and card drop options for everyone worldwide.

Can I play this at school or work?

Yes. AetherForge is an unblocked browser game — it requires no installation, no external resources beyond fonts, and no network connection after the initial page load.

Is it similar to Balatro or Slay the Spire?

It shares the core DNA: elemental synergies, a run-based boss gauntlet, and deck-building reward choices. It is lighter and faster — designed for a single daily session rather than a multi-hour run.
