How to Play Daily Word Scramble

Each day a new word is chosen using a globally synchronized seed — the same word for every player worldwide. The letters are shuffled and displayed above. You have 6 attempts to guess the correct word.

Colour Clues

  • Green — correct letter, correct position
  • Yellow — letter is in the word but wrong position
  • Grey — letter not in the word

After you finish, copy your spoiler-free emoji result and share it with friends to compare without revealing the answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this free to play?

Yes — completely free, no login required. Open the page and play instantly.

How is it different from Wordle?

You can see all the letters in the word upfront — they're just scrambled. This makes it a different skill: pattern recognition and anagramming rather than pure deduction.

Does it reset daily?

Yes. Every day at midnight UTC the seed advances to the new date, generating a fresh word for everyone worldwide.

Is progress saved?

Yes — your current game state is saved in your browser's localStorage so you can close the tab and come back.

Can I play at school or work?

Yes. It's an unblocked browser word game — no installation, no external content, runs offline after first load.
