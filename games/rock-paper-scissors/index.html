How to Play

Tap Rock, Paper, or Scissors to make your move. The CPU responds instantly. First to win 3 rounds wins the series (best of 5). Draws don't count toward the series total.

Easy vs Hard CPU

Easy — the CPU picks completely at random. Pure 50/50 luck.
Hard — the CPU tracks which moves you've played and uses a frequency-weighted counter to predict your next move and pick the one that beats it. It's not unbeatable, but it will punish patterns and habits. Mix up your moves!
